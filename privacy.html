Privacy policy – Gestion Transport Pro and online shop

Who we are

The Gestion Transport Pro app is published by SALOUM UNIVERS SERVICES GROUPE SARL, Parcelles Assainies Unité 4, Villa N° 186, Guédiawaye, Dakar, Senegal (trade register SN.DKR.2026.B.10704, NINEA 012911922). Each client company remains the owner of the data it records in the app; we host and process it only to run the service.

Data we store

  • Account: name, e-mail address or phone number, role in the company.
  • Company data: vehicles, drivers, customers, trips, invoices, payments, expenses, fuel vouchers, salaries.
  • Photos taken in the app: receipts and fuel pump meter.
  • GPS location of a driver's phone, only during a mission (see below).
  • Activity log within the company, and technical crash reports.

GPS location

The location of a driver's phone is recorded only during a mission, after the driver taps “Start trip”, so that the company can follow the trip. A “Mission tracking” notification stays visible the whole time. It is also read when refuelling, to suggest the nearest station. Tracking stops when the driver returns or the mission ends. No location is collected outside a mission.

Why we use this data

Only to provide the service: manage trips, compute expenses and profits, produce invoices, approve fuel vouchers, send useful notifications to the company and fix app errors. Data is never sold, rented or used for advertising.

Who can see the data

Only members of the company concerned, according to their role (CEO, manager, driver, viewer). A driver only sees their own entries and missions. We do not share data with third parties, except with our technical providers below or when required by law.

Where it is stored

On Google Firebase servers (database, authentication and photo storage), over encrypted connections. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) are used only to fix the app. The password remembered on the phone is encrypted by the Android system.

Retention

Data remains available as long as the company uses the app. After a deletion request, it is permanently erased within 30 days.

Your rights

Under Senegalese law No. 2008-12 on the protection of personal data, you can ask to access, correct or delete your data. In the app: My account › Delete my account. Or by e-mail: sunsgroupesarl@gmail.com. See also Delete an account.

Online shop

When you place an order or a quote request on the shop, we store your name, phone number, delivery address, ordered products and notes. This information is used only to prepare, deliver and follow up your order and to call you about it. It is never sold or used for advertising. It is stored on Google Firebase servers and only our team can access it. The cart is kept on your phone (browser storage). Orders are kept for 3 years for our accounting; you can ask for earlier deletion at sunsgroupesarl@gmail.com.

Children

The app is intended for companies and their employees. It is not intended for people under 18.
