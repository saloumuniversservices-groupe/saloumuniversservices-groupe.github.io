Delete an account – Gestion Transport Pro

From the app

  1. Open Gestion Transport Pro and sign in.
  2. Tap your account icon at the top right (or the ⋮ menu for a driver).
  3. Tap Delete my account, then type SUPPRIMER to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Driver, manager or viewer: your access and login are deleted immediately. Entries already made (expenses, refuels, missions) remain in the company's records, which it needs for its accounting.
  • CEO: the request covers the whole company (vehicles, trips, invoices, team accounts, photos). All this data is permanently erased within 30 days. During this period you can still cancel by writing to us.

Without the app

Send an e-mail to sunsgroupesarl@gmail.com with the company name and the e-mail address or phone number of the account. We confirm the deletion within 30 days.

Invoices already issued may have to be kept by the client company under its legal obligations; please download them before deletion.
